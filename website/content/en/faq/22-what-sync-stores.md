---
title: "What does sync save? Are my settings synced?"
weight: 220
---

With iCloud, Dropbox or Google Drive, FeedFlow saves a file in an app-specific folder of your storage (it can't see your other files) containing your feeds, categories, and read and bookmarked articles. Article content isn't included.

With Folder, the same data goes into a backup file in a folder you choose, for example one shared with Syncthing. You decide where the file lives and which tool moves it between the devices.

Settings, blocked words and widget configuration stay on each device and aren't synced. The sync file isn't encrypted by FeedFlow, so it's protected by your storage account, or by the permissions of the folder you pick.

With a reader service (FreshRSS, Miniflux, BazQux, Feedbin), what's synced is decided by the service.
